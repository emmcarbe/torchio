Model for the edition, not for the viewer.

Torchio presses XML-TEI P5 encoded texts into static digital scholarly editions: multi-page sites with reading text, critical apparatus, indices, maps and data exports, published from a git repository, with no server and no database. It is a prototype under development, and an experiment: how much of the editorial behaviour of real TEI can be recovered without configuration, and without inventing what the source does not say.

The press, in the browser

Press an edition here: choose the files of a TEI P5 edition (the ODD too, if you have one) and it is pressed into a static site on your machine. Compose it in the panel, add your own pages in Markdown, download the result. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, and nothing is kept.

Editions to look at

  • The demonstration editions: nine editions pressed with Torchio, in their own repository, so that materials with limited rights stay apart from the engine. Among them a critical edition with a three-register apparatus, a collated tradition of fifty-four witnesses, a graphematic transcription, a documentary transcription with hands and writing operations, and a correspondence.
  • The Specimen: a micro-edition built for demonstration, with imaginary witnesses and didactic variants, declared as such on its title page. CC0, so anyone may take it apart.
